## Important distinctions

- The preview is connected to the real .NET API, but the running persistence adapter is the development-only in-memory Demo adapter. Restarting the API resets synthetic data.
- The SQL adapter compiles and migration 0004 passes static TSQL parsing. No SQL Server instance was available in this environment, so no claim is made that Phase 4 migrations or queries have executed successfully. `tests/Careflow.SqlTests` runs the Phase 1–4 database gate in the CI `sql-integration` job.
- **No live payer/TPA gateway, TPA portal, video/RTC provider, payment/bank service or procurement marketplace is integrated or claimed.** The telemedicine "join token" is Careflow's own short-lived authorization token for the consultation workflow — it is not a video room, and the sessions page says so explicitly.
- Insurance settlements are recorded against approved amounts with cumulative caps; they are ledger entries, not bank transactions. Incentive payments are payout records against approved batches, not payment-service instructions.
- Incentive attribution is deterministic and auditable: each line snapshots the basis amount, the matched rule and its mode/value at calculation time. Revenue share attributes invoice payments to the doctor of the latest signed encounter at or before the payment time; payments without a signed encounter are skipped rather than guessed.
- Separation of duties is enforced: the user who calculates an incentive batch cannot approve it, and the user who raises an inventory indent cannot approve it. Self-approval attempts fail with `incentive.separation` / `inventory.separation` and are audited.
- CSSD release requires at least one passing chemical indicator, no failed indicator and no missing instruments; indicator results are append-only and release with an open failed indicator is refused. Sterile expiry is 30 days (180 for EO) from release.
- Workspace and chart care lists are bounded and expose truncation warnings. They are not complete archives.
