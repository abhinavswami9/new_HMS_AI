## Important distinctions

- The **preview is connected to a real .NET API**, but its persistence adapter is development-only and in-memory. It is not browser-local fake CRUD. Restarting the API resets synthetic data.
- The SQL adapter compiles, and the migration passes static TSQL parsing. No SQL Server instance was available, so no claim is made that migrations or SQL queries have executed successfully.
- Allergy snapshots currently belong to an encounter. They are not yet a reconciled longitudinal allergy registry. The UI explicitly warns that no recorded allergy does not mean no allergy.
- Workspace lists are bounded to 200 recent records and expose a truncation warning. Patient charts are queried by patient and expose a 200-per-category history limit. Complete archive pagination remains a Phase 1 task; never interpret a truncated view as a complete clinical history.
- Signed records are locked in this slice. Addendum/correction workflows are planned; do not work around immutability by creating an unrelated duplicate record.
- The signature hash binds canonical clinical content but does not establish a trusted certificate identity. Printouts say electronically recorded and identify synthetic records.
- CSV exports are convenience exports of the current authorized view, not the future governed report builder.

These checks are development evidence, not a production readiness or accreditation assessment.
